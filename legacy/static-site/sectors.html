Infrastructure Sectors

Monitoring and assessment approaches tailored to the physical behaviour, operating conditions and risks of each asset class.

Dams and reservoirs

Concrete, masonry, earth and rockfill dams; spillways, intakes, galleries, barrages, weirs and hydropower structures.

  • Seepage, uplift and pore pressure
  • Displacement, settlement and joints
  • Reservoir-level and temperature effects
  • Instrumentation and monitoring reviews

Bridges and viaducts

Highway, railway, prestressed, steel and cable-supported bridges.

  • Deflection, strain and vibration
  • Bearing and expansion-joint movement
  • Modal response and fatigue-support data
  • Scour and foundation observation

Buildings

Public, industrial, high-rise, heritage and construction-affected structures.

  • Crack, settlement and tilt monitoring
  • Vibration and seismic response
  • Excavation- and construction-stage monitoring
  • Condition assessment

Other critical assets

Tunnels, slopes, retaining walls, pipelines, marine and industrial structures.

  • Geotechnical monitoring
  • Remote sensing and survey integration
  • Risk-based inspection support
  • Project-specific monitoring systems

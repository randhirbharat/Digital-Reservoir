Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about structural health monitoring, digital twins, HST analysis and consultancy services.

What is structural health monitoring?

It is the systematic use of inspection, sensors, measurements, data analysis and engineering models to understand infrastructure condition and behaviour.

Can monitoring data prove structural safety?

No. Monitoring data are one part of an evidence-based assessment. They must be reviewed with inspections, operating history, sensor quality and professional engineering judgement.

Does an anomaly prove damage?

No. It may result from sensor fault, data errors, operational changes, environmental effects or physical change. It requires verification.

Can Digital Reservoir work with existing instruments?

Yes. Existing sensor systems, data loggers and records can be reviewed for functionality, calibration, data quality and upgrade opportunities.

Is public dashboard data real?

No. Website demonstrations use synthetic or authorised anonymised data and do not describe any real asset safety status.

Can AI predict structural failure?

No model can guarantee failure prediction. Models can support anomaly screening and maintenance prioritisation, subject to engineering review.

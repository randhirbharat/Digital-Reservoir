Digital Reservoir — one identity, three applications

A restrained engineering identity: reservoir navy and petroleum teal for authority, warm ivory and mist sage for long-form reading, muted bronze and soft aqua as measured accents. The mark is a gravity-dam section holding a reservoir, crossed by a monitoring signal that ends on the structure.
